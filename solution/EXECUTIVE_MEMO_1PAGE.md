# CRED Collections — Executive Memo (1 page, for the CEO)

**To:** CEO | **From:** Collections Analytics | **Date:** 9 Sep 2026
**Subject:** March did not grow 11%. It was flat. Here's where ₹10 Cr should go.

## What happened?

March looked like +11% only because February has 28 days and March has 31. Adjusted for days, growth was **essentially zero — statistically flat**. We also worked 10% more accounts in March to earn 1% more per account. Call-answer and promise-kept rates were flat all year.

**Use ₹126.8 Cr (Jan–Jul, cleaned) as the revenue base**, not the raw number (which overstates by ~₹2.9 Cr of duplicates).

## Why?

More calendar days + more accounts worked + slightly easier account mix. No evidence of better execution. Our current account-prioritisation also shows **no ability to pick winners** (top-priority and bottom-priority accounts convert identically), and accounts we never contacted convert no worse than ones we did.

## What should we do?

Put the ₹10 Cr into **better borrower targeting (#4) — as a capped experiment, not a sure bet**:

- Build a new propensity score and fix promise follow-up (**~₹1.2 Cr estimate**).
- Test it on half the volume for 8–12 weeks against current logic (**go/no-go gate**).
- **Stop at 4 weeks if lift is under 3% — loss capped at ₹1.2 Cr.**

Scenarios: **pessimistic ₹0** (test fails, lose ₹1.2 Cr) · **base +₹4–6 Cr/yr** (modest lift, 2–4×) · **upside +₹10–15 Cr/yr** (strong lift, holdout-confirmed only).
